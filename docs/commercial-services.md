## Usage

| Scenario | Permission |
| --- | --- |
| Personal learning, research and everyday use | Free |
| An individual's independently developed project, including one that earns revenue | Free, excluding resale of this Software or hosted automation services for enterprises |
| Work for an employer or an enterprise's internal operations | Paid enterprise license |
| Consulting or contract work on behalf of an enterprise | Commercial permission covering the actual organization and service scope |
| Enterprise evaluation | Arrange written evaluation permission and its term |
| Other organizational use | Arrange permission and fees separately |

The purpose of use determines eligibility. A personal device or account does
not turn enterprise work into personal use. Internal business use also requires
permission. Source visibility does not grant unrestricted commercial rights;
the LICENSE or a separate written agreement defines the applicable terms.
